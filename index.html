About Me

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, advised by Prof. Ke Yi and Prof. Nian Si. My research focuses on reinforcement learning, machine learning theory, applied probability, and game theory.

Before my PhD, I received a BSc in Mathematics and Computer Science from HKUST with First Class Honors. I am broadly interested in building theoretically grounded learning algorithms for sequential decision-making problems, with recent work on average-reward reinforcement learning, distributionally robust RL, and privacy-preserving data systems.
